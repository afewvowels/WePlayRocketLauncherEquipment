Version: 0.3.3
Date: 2026. 11. 02
  Features:
    - The launcher can now fire any rocket ammo, including explosive rockets, atomic bombs and modded rockets. It loads the best rocket type in the inventory, and the loaded ammo shows on the launcher in the armor grid.
    - Added a per-player rocket priority setting.
    - Added a per-player setting to allow atomic rockets (off by default), and a startup setting for the atomic minimum range.

  Changes:
    - Renamed the item, equipment and recipe to wp-personal-rocket-launcher-equipment to avoid name collisions with other mods. Existing saves are migrated.
